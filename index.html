1. Metaphors for multicultural societies

Metaphors are simplified images that describe complex societies. They can highlight diversity, but none gives a complete or neutral picture.

Checklist

Key vocabulary and phrases

  • assimilation — Assimilation
  • integration — Integration

4. Equality and progress

Central idea: legal or political progress matters, but it does not automatically create equal treatment and opportunity in everyday life.

Checklist

Key vocabulary and phrases

  • legal equality — rechtliche Gleichheit
  • lived equality — tatsächlich erfahrene Gleichheit
  • equal opportunity — Chancengleichheit
  • political representation — politische Repräsentation
  • systemic injustice — systemische Ungerechtigkeit
  • equal treatment — Gleichbehandlung

6. Answer structure and final check

Strong answers combine accurate knowledge, subject vocabulary, evidence and a balanced judgement.

Checklist

Key vocabulary and phrases

  • The picture shows …
  • This detail suggests that …
  • The use of … emphasises …
  • This connects to our lesson on … because …
  • However, it also shows continuity because …
  • A balanced interpretation is that …
Main course idea: Societies may be culturally diverse, but diversity alone does not guarantee equal rights, power, representation or opportunities. The history of slavery, emancipation, segregation and civil-rights struggles demonstrates both major progress and continuing inequalities.
